## Sources & Licences

### OpenStreetMap (ODbL)

**Licence**: Open Database Licence (ODbL) v1.0 — <https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/>

4,590 rows in `atm_branch_agent_locations.csv` are sourced from OpenStreetMap via the HOT/HDX
India POI export (Geofabrik IND snapshot 2026-05-10, retrieved from data.humdata.org).
These rows have `source = osm` (original) or `source = synthetic` (where OSM coordinates were
corrected to fix geocoding errors; see Hamirpur UP notes).

### Census of India 2011

District population, area, and urbanisation data in `districts.csv` (under `data/seed/`) is
sourced from the Census 2011 Primary Census Abstract, published by the Office of the Registrar
General & Census Commissioner, India (censusindia.gov.in). This is public-domain government data.

### Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) — Parliamentary Replies

State-level cybercrime complaint volumes in `state_cybercrime_weights.csv` are from:
- MHA Rajya Sabha Unstarred Question No. 1200, 30 July 2025 (NCRP incident counts by state, 2024).
- MHA Lok Sabha Unstarred Question No. 344, 22 July 2025 (total financial fraud losses 2024).
- MHA Rajya Sabha Unstarred Question No. 1505, 12 March 2025 (digital arrest incidents 2024).

All MHA parliamentary replies are public domain (parliament.nic.in / prsindia.org).

### Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Bank names and types in `banks.csv` are sourced from the RBI list of scheduled commercial banks
(rbi.org.in). Reflects the 1 May 2025 One State-One RRB amalgamations. Public domain.

### NPCI

AePS per-transaction and monthly caps in `sim_parameter_calibration.csv` are sourced from:
- NPCI AePS Product FAQs (npci.org.in)
- NPCI Circular OC 87, 2 November 2023 (monthly cumulative cash withdrawal cap: ₹50,000)

Public domain regulatory information.

### Bundled GeoJSON Boundaries (`demo_states.geojson`)
- **Source**: Public domain boundary approximations derived from Survey of India public state boundary outlines, simplified to polygon geometries suitable for offline, bundle-safe rendering (<50KB total footprint).
- **Licence**: ODC-By 1.0 / Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India compatible.
- **Coverage**: Four demo states (UP, MH, HR, JH — JH replaces RJ as of this data drop, since the
  seed CSVs above only cover these four). The JH polygon is a hand-simplified approximation at
  the same ~7-8 vertex precision as the other three (not traced from a precision boundary
  dataset — no such offline dataset was available); good enough for the Map's fill/outline
  rendering, not for anything geometrically exact.
- **NFR Compliance (DOC 2 §2.7)**: Zero external CDN or tile-server network requests at runtime; offline-ready and bundled directly into client distributions.

## Synthetic Fills

3,914 rows in `atm_branch_agent_locations.csv` have `source = synthetic`. These are:
- Rural district gap-fills (districts with fewer than 5 OSM points topped up to minimum coverage).
- AePS/BC AGENT points (no public agent-point registry exists in India; these are
  plausible simulated points distributed across rural tehsils).
- 4 Hamirpur UP location coordinates corrected from a geocoding error (original OSM export
  picked up Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh instead of Hamirpur, Uttar Pradesh).
